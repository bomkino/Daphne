- **Instagram:** upload the PNGs from the folder in order (`launch-01.png` first). Write the alt text for each image before posting. Links in captions don't click, so the bio link should be `pitch.dog/daphne` for the two weeks.
- **On each posting day,** share the post to Stories with a link sticker to `pitch.dog/daphne`.
- **LinkedIn:** post the folder's PDF as a document (`daphne-<name>-linkedin.pdf`) and give it the document title below. The link can sit in the caption.
- Nothing here has been posted, scheduled or sent anywhere.

> Six things to know before a laurel goes on your poster:
>
> 1. Ask the festival first. Many send official laurels, and some require theirs.
> 2. Say exactly what you got, in the festival's own wording.
> 3. Wait for the announcement before you post.
> 4. Fewer, stronger laurels: wins, then premieres, then selections.
> 5. Keep them readable: one size across a row.
> 6. Watch for look-alikes: some events exist to sell laurels.

## What the words rest on

Every number and claim above was checked against the app's own code on October 31, 2026:
78 looks; 268 festivals from 60 countries; the common statuses in English, French, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese;
PNG, SVG, PDF and MP4 downloads; the email read in the browser; the festival pack's colorways, formats and GUIDELINES.txt;
the six tips, which are the app's own guide. The myth follows Ovid's *Metamorphoses* (Book 1); laurel wreaths at the
Pythian Games at Delphi, held in Apollo's honor and first built around music and poetry; *laureate* from Latin
*laureātus*, crowned with laurel; Greek *daphne*, laurel. No real festival is named or shown anywhere.
