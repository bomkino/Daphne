- **Instagram:** upload the PNGs from the folder in order (`launch-01.png` first). Write the alt text for each image before posting. Links in captions don't click, so the bio link should be `pitch.dog/daphne` while these run.
- **On each posting day,** share the post to Stories with a link sticker to `pitch.dog/daphne`.
- **LinkedIn:** post the folder's PDF as a document (`daphne-<name>-linkedin.pdf`) and give it the document title below. The link can sit in the caption.
- Nothing here has been posted, scheduled or sent anywhere.

## What the words rest on

Every number and claim above was checked against the app's own code on October 31, 2026:
78 looks; 268 festivals from 60 countries; the common statuses in English, French, Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese;
PNG, SVG, PDF and MP4 downloads; the email read in the browser; the festival pack's colorways, formats and GUIDELINES.txt;
the six tips, which are the app's own guide. The myth follows Ovid's *Metamorphoses* (Book 1); laurel wreaths at the
Pythian Games at Delphi, held in Apollo's honor and first built around music and poetry; *laureate* from Latin
*laureātus*, crowned with laurel; Greek *daphne*, laurel. No real festival is named or shown anywhere.

For 06 to 15, checked against the app and research/01 on November 2, 2026: the contrast On poster reports (1.1:1 for gold
on the wheat-field poster, 8.7:1 with Auto ink) and the four colorways it suggests, captured from the app; autosave in
the browser and the share link that holds the whole laurel (Share link, top right); the clean-up for festival files
(white or black taken out, one ink, trimmed) and its note on recoloring; the social card's four shapes, its photo
background and the first line it writes; the ten badges in the Badges group; the motion end card (30 fps, 120 frames,
4 seconds; MP4, transparent PNG sequence, animated SVG; HD, 4K, square, portrait, vertical); 18 layouts. From research/01:
16 of 42 festival-issued laurels have no branches, and the top tier now uses logo badges; the year has its own line on 28
of 68; no laurel we studied carried two awards. The six posters are made up, drawn for these slides; the laurels on them
are Daphne's own looks.
