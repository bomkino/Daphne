# Public domain parts of Daphne

Daphne is as open as we can make it. The code is MIT (see `LICENSE`). Everything else we made is dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (`CC0-1.0`): copy it, change it and use it, commercially too, without asking and without credit.

Legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>

## What's CC0

- **The looks and layouts:** the presets in `prototype/presets.js` and the layouts in `prototype/layouts.js`.
- **The festival data:** the festival list in `prototype/festivals.js`, the laurel rules in `prototype/rules.js`, and the status words and translations in `prototype/words.js`. Where a file holds both data and code, the data is CC0 and the code is MIT.
- **The brand:** everything in `brand/` (see `brand/LICENSE.md`) and the copies of it in `prototype/assets/`, including the PD fonts, which are CC0 by pitch.dog's own declaration (`prototype/assets/fonts/FONT-LICENSE.md`).
- **The writing:** the plans, the research notes and this documentation. Quotes from festivals' own rules stay theirs; our summaries and the links to their sources are CC0.
- **What you make:** Daphne claims no rights in the laurels, cards, end cards or files people make with it. What Daphne draws is dedicated to the public domain, so nobody owes us credit. The festival's name and any artwork you add belong to their owners.

## What isn't ours to give

- **Festival names, logos and laurels** belong to the festivals. Daphne ships no festival artwork. A laurel made with Daphne is the filmmaker's own claim, not an endorsement by the festival.
- **Fonts used inside laurels** come from Google Fonts under open licenses (almost all the SIL Open Font License). They're free to use, commercially too, under their own terms.
- **The vendored libraries** in `prototype/vendor/` keep their own MIT licenses.

## Trademarks

CC0 leaves trademarks out of its waiver. We don't claim the Daphne name or its marks as trademarks, so use them freely. Two requests, never conditions: don't use them to make something look like pitch.dog made or endorsed it when we didn't, and if Daphne helped, "Made with Daphne" is a kind thing to say.
