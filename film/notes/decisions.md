# Daphne's story film: decisions

*One line per decision, newest first: the date, the decision, why, and who made it.*

- 2026-10-31 The film ends on the intro/outro's outro, cut in on its first frame, and our last laurel takes the sting's word sizes so nothing moves at the cut. Why: one ending for every Daphne film; the sting's laurel set its words smaller (it measured type in Node). (us)

- 2026-10-31 On-screen words arrive at 0.8× speaking time in the text cuts. Why: reading runs ahead of speech, and it leaves each line time to be read whole. (us)
- 2026-10-31 Score mixed with a slow leveler (halves the gap between quiet and loud sections) and a heartbeat with harmonics. Why: the first mix had the prologue 28 dB under the chase, and a 40–64 Hz heartbeat a phone speaker can't play. (us)
- 2026-10-31 The score is our own, written in code and played on VSCO 2: Community Edition (CC0). Why: the brief allows only CC0 or our own music; a sampled orchestra sounds warmer than pure synthesis. (us)
