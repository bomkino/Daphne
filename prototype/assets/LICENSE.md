# Assets

These are copies of Daphne's brand files from `brand/`, for the app to load: the logo, the favicons and app icons, the social card, the leaf brackets, and the PD fonts trimmed to Latin and to the weights the app uses.

All of them are in the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (`CC0-1.0`). See `brand/LICENSE.md` and `fonts/FONT-LICENSE.md`. Change a file in `brand/` first and copy it here, so the two stay the same.
